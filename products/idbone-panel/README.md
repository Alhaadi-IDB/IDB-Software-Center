**Your workspace for everyday IDB tasks.**

## Getting started

1. Open the IDBOne Panel link provided by your organization.
2. Sign in with your assigned account.
3. Choose a section from the menu to begin.

If you have trouble signing in, contact your IDB system administrator or support team.

## What you can do

### Leave requests

Create a leave request, save a draft, and review your requests under **My Leave Request**. Use search and filters to find requests by year, status, or leave dates.

If you are an approver, **Need Approval** shows requests assigned to you. Staff with leave management access can also use **Pending** and **Finalize**. Available actions depend on each request's status and your access. You can review leave balances and prepare leave reports where available.

### Calendar and ONE Event

Use the calendar to see scheduled activities and leave. If you manage ONE Events, you can create events, add speakers and event details, and manage attendance and feedback.

Participants can use event check-in and check-out forms when provided by an organizer. Event managers can prepare PDF or Excel reports; including signature images may take longer.

### Event certificates

Qualified participants can request a certificate from an event's certificate page. **Pending approval** means an authorized reviewer needs to approve the request. After approval and preparation, the certificate can be opened when its PDF is available.

Authorized certificate managers can review requests in **Certificate Management**, approve eligible requests individually or in a group, open issued certificates, and revoke a certificate with a reason. Previously issued certificates may be reused to avoid duplicates.

### Assets, incidents, and terminations

Where your role allows it, use these sections to create and review records, add attachments, search listings, and prepare reports. Dashboards and filters help you find the information you need.

### Messages, tickets, and templates

Use messages and tickets to communicate and follow up on support matters. You can review unread items, missed calls, and ticket status. If you have access to message templates, you can prepare, preview, schedule, and monitor messages for selected recipients or groups.

### Account and access

Your profile and menu options reflect your assigned access. Two-factor authentication may be available for your account. Ask your administrator if you need a feature that is not shown.
